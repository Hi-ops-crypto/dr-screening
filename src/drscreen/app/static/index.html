Scoring

    The tier combines published risk associations (Yau et al., Diabetes Care 2012). Its cut-offs are a design choice, not a validated clinical score.

    How it works

    Every photo takes the same path. A rejected photo stops at the first step with a reason.

    1. Quality gate

      MobileNetV3 trained on EyeQ labels sorts the photo into good, usable or reject.

    2. Grading

      EfficientNet-B3 trained on about 41,000 photos from APTOS, EyePACS and DDR reads the ICDR grade, 0 to 4.

    3. Heatmap

      Grad-CAM shows which regions drove the grade. It shows where the model looked, not where lesions are.

    4. Referral

      Grade 2 or worse, or a referable chance above a threshold tuned for 95% sensitivity, means refer.
